# Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard \| Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project is an interactive **Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard built
in Power BI** to analyze sales performance across different regions,
products, retailers, and months.

The dashboard focuses on turning sales data into clear business insights
and making it easier to identify trends and high-performing segments.

## 🎯 Objectives

-   Analyze overall sales and profitability
-   Track monthly sales trends
-   Compare sales performance across regions
-   Identify top-performing product categories
-   Compare retailer performance
-   Understand key business KPIs
-   Create an interactive and easy-to-use Power BI report

## 📌 Key KPIs

The dashboard highlights:

-   **Total Sales:** \$900M
-   **Operating Profit:** \$332M
-   **Units Sold:** 2M
-   **Average Price:** \$45
-   **Operating Margin:** 42%

## 🔍 Key Insights

### Monthly Sales

-   **July** recorded the highest monthly sales at approximately
    **\$95M**.
-   **March** recorded the lowest monthly sales at approximately
    **\$57M**.
-   Sales increased strongly from March through July before declining
    during the following months.

### Regional Performance

-   The **West region** was the strongest contributor, generating
    approximately **\$270M**, representing **30% of total sales**.
-   Northeast and Southeast also made significant contributions to
    overall sales.

### Product Performance

-   **Men's Street Footwear** was the highest-performing product
    category, generating approximately **\$209M**.
-   Women's Apparel and Men's Athletic Footwear were also among the
    leading categories.

### Retailer Performance

-   **West Gear** generated the highest sales among the retailers at
    approximately **\$243M**.
-   **Foot Locker** followed closely with approximately **\$220M**.
-   Sports Direct ranked third with approximately **\$182M**.
